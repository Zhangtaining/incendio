What is a real-time AI avatar for live selling?

A real-time AI avatar for live selling is a virtual host that can present products, respond to viewer questions, and guide shoppers during a livestream. Unlike pre-recorded avatar videos, it connects live comments, product knowledge, speech generation, avatar animation, and operator controls into one workflow.

FAQ

Answers for buyers, operators, and technical evaluators.

Can the AI avatar answer customer questions in real time?

Yes. The system is designed to ingest live comments, classify intent, retrieve product facts, generate a sales-aware response, synthesize speech, and animate the avatar. Sensitive responses can require operator approval.

How is this different from a pre-recorded avatar video?

Pre-recorded video repeats a fixed message. Incendio Avatar is designed for interactive selling: viewer comments, product knowledge, risk checks, human takeover, and post-live learning all happen around the avatar.

Can it work with TikTok Shop or Shopify sellers?

The product is designed for social commerce and e-commerce workflows, including TikTok Shop-style selling and Shopify catalog-driven demos. Specific platform connectors depend on rollout stage and account requirements.

Does the system include compliance controls?

Incendio Avatar includes a risk preflight concept for claims, sensitive words, repeated scripts, disclosure language, and output readiness. Operators can also take over whenever judgment is needed.

What integrations are planned?

The roadmap includes OBS, browser source, virtual camera, RTMP output, product catalog import, live chat ingestion, and commerce platform workflows for sellers and agencies.

Who should join the beta?

Online sellers, TikTok Shop-style operators, Shopify merchants, agencies, and brands that want frequent live product demos without keeping a human host on camera all day.
